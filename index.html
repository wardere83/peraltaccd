Program Spotlight — LIFT Mentor Match Program

Peralta CCD is joining the multi-region LIFT Alliance to launch a Mentor Match Program across Berkeley City, Alameda, Laney, and Merritt. The program pairs structured Mentor & Match platform training with peer mentorship and closes a post-hire gap in Peralta’s 2024–2027 EEO Plan by institutionalizing leadership and retention strategies.

GOAL 1Instructional Tech Access Across Colleges

Expand equitable access to professional development through mentorship across all four Peralta colleges, with intentional inclusion of classified staff and part-time faculty.

Activities

  • Stand up a districtwide cohort using the Mentor & Match Professional Platform so classified, part-time, and full-time employees at Berkeley City, Alameda, Laney, and Merritt have equal access to mentor pairing.
  • Stand up Mentor & Match Professional Platform accounts for all four PCCD colleges; cross-campus mentor pairing across Berkeley City, Alameda, Laney, and Merritt so classified, part-time, and full-time employees have equal access.
  • Deliver six 4-hour Mentor & Match platform sessions in Fall 2026 covering hardware, best practices, inclusive lesson design, and collaborative curriculum development.
  • Pilot first mentor cohorts in 2025-26 and present results at the LIFT Mentorship Impact Showcase & Summit alongside Feather River and Sacramento City College.

Measurable Outcomes & Assessment

  1. 01
    At least 30 faculty trained across the three colleges, with participation proportional to each campus’s FTES (±5%).
    AssessmentSign-in sheets, cohort rosters disaggregated by campus.
  2. 02
    ≥ 35% of participants represent non-STEM disciplines.
    AssessmentCohort discipline tagging at enrollment.
  3. 03
    Five published mentoring stories per college in the Mentorship Reflection Journal (20 total across PCCD); 50% of employees opt into the Mentor & Match Professional Platform.
    AssessmentStandardized lesson template completion logs & cross-campus adoption tracking.
GOAL 2Faculty Leadership Pipeline & Post-Hire EEO

Build faculty leadership through inclusive Mentor & Match platform and address a critical gap in Peralta CCD’s 2024–2027 EEO Plan by adding sustained post-hire professional development and retention strategies.

Activities

  • Pair every cohort participant with a trained peer mentor for individualized guidance and troubleshooting.
  • Promote Year-1 mentees into mentor roles in Year 2; introduce Culture-of-Care Awards and District Staff Stipends to deepen classified leadership pipelines.
  • Equip mentors and mentees with shared-goal pairing, lived-experience matching, and reflection tools that foster empathy, cultural understanding, and inclusive leadership.
  • Document leadership growth in participant portfolios (committee roles, coordinator duties, presentations) and feed results into Peralta CCD’s EEO Plan refresh.

Measurable Outcomes & Assessment

  1. 01
    ≥ 75% of mentees report increased confidence and career-direction clarity (pre/post survey); ≥ 60% complete at least one structured mentoring cycle within the grant year.
    AssessmentPre/post participant surveys, lesson implementation logs.
  2. 02
    ≥ 40% of Year-1 participants advance to mentor roles in Year 2; ≥ 25% of alumni document new leadership/service.
    AssessmentMentor program rosters, professional-portfolio audits.
  3. 03
    In participating sections, ≥ 70% of students report increased engagement, with no engagement gap > 10 percentage points between student demographic groups.
    AssessmentPost-lesson student pulse surveys disaggregated by race / gender.
How the goals connect
Application Narrative · Section 1.3

Together, these goals form a single districtwide approach: equitable access to mentorship paired with a leadership pipeline that institutionalizes post-hire professional development. The program runs across all four Peralta colleges — Berkeley City, College of Alameda, Laney, and Merritt — so employee growth no longer depends on campus assignment or job classification.

Two Project Goals
From the application workplan, Section 2.1
GOAL 1 Instructional Tech Access Across Colleges
Close instructional technology access gaps across the three Peralta colleges

Provision Mentor & Match Professional Platform accounts at Berkeley City, Alameda, Laney, and Merritt so classified staff, part-time employees, and historically underrepresented groups have equal access to mentorship and leadership development.

GOAL 2 Faculty Leadership Pipeline & Post-Hire EEO
Build a districtwide leadership pipeline through Mentor & Match platform & peer mentoring

Train classified, faculty, and administrator mentors in inclusive matching, expand Year-1 mentees into mentor roles in Year 2, and close the post-hire mentorship gap in PCCD’s 2024–2027 EEO Plan.

Workplan Activities
By project year · from Section 2.2
YEAR 1 · 2026–2027
  • Mentor onboarding curriculum. Mentor and mentee training sessions covering the Mentor & Match Professional Platform, equitable pairing best practices, classified leadership growth, and reflection tools.
  • Peer mentoring pairs. Each participant pairs with a trained peer mentor for individualized application of Mentor Match strategies.
  • Classroom implementation. Spring 2027 pilot of mentor-paired lessons across the three colleges.
  • End-of-year showcase. Districtwide internal conference; standardized lesson documentation captured for the shared catalog.
YEAR 2 · 2027–2028
  • Mentor program expansion. New mentees plus returning participants advancing into mentor roles.
  • Year 2 / Year 3 mentor relaunch. Returning mentors and mentees engage in deeper pairing, Culture-of-Care Awards, and the LIFT Mentorship Impact Showcase & Summit.
  • Faculty Innovation Stipends. Larger-scale Mentor Match projects supported by stipends drawn from a dedicated pool.
  • Final showcase & living catalog. Districtwide presentation; documented lessons organized into a searchable, continuously updated repository across all three colleges.
Institutional Resources Used
  • Office of Planning & Educational Technology. Owns Mentor & Match platform accounts procurement, faculty cohort logistics, and the shared mentorship session catalog.
  • Berkeley City College, College of Alameda, Laney College, & Merritt College. Each campus contributes a classified liaison and a cohort of mentors/mentees pairing via the Mentor & Match Professional Platform.
  • Faculty leaders & Mentor Match subject-matter experts. Anchor the peer mentorship model and deliver the six-session training series.
  • District EEO Committee. Aligns the program with the 2024–2027 EEO Plan and shepherds the post-hire professional-development update.
  • Institutional Research / Office of Educational Technology. Data collection, disaggregated analysis, and lesson-catalog infrastructure.
Five Mentor Match Focus Areas

The program is structured around five domains drawn from the project workplan and CCCCO 2022 EEO/Diversity Best Practices Handbook:

  • Equitable mentor/mentee matching on the Mentor & Match Professional Platform
  • Classified-staff leadership pipeline and campus liaison coordination
  • Cross-classification pairing across faculty, classified, and administrators
  • Mentorship reflection, story-collection, and Mentorship Reflection Journal
  • Multi-region LIFT alliance impact study, summit, and shared learnings
